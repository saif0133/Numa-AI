What reaches this team

  1. Urgent alerts the treating clinician has not acknowledged in time, or that arrive outside working hours.
  2. Chats a patient chose to report, with only the entries the AI used in that chat.

Protocol (written with specialists)

  1. Try the treating clinician again.
  2. Contact the patient's emergency contact if one is on file.
  3. If there is immediate danger, call emergency services: 911 in Jordan, 999 or 998 in the UAE.
  4. Every view of patient content is logged.
